Complimentary Meet & Greet

Start With a Conversation.

No commitment. No enrollment forms. Just a conversation.

If you're considering Shipley Pediatrics for your child's primary care, schedule a complimentary Meet & Greet with Jennifer Shipley, CPNP, to learn about Pediatric Direct Primary Care, ask questions, and see if Shipley is the right fit for your family.

Choose a time Learn about Pediatric DPC first

What to expect

A brief, non-clinical introduction.

This brief, non-clinical conversation gives you an opportunity to learn about Pediatric Direct Primary Care, ask questions, meet Jen, and decide whether Shipley Pediatrics feels like the right fit for your family.

  • Learn about the membership, pricing, and approach to care
  • Ask questions about what you're looking for in your child's pediatric care
  • Meet Jen and get a feel for the practice
  • Decide whether Shipley Pediatrics feels like the right fit — no pressure

A Meet & Greet is intended as a non-clinical introductory conversation and is not a medical appointment.

The Meet & Greet is specifically for families interested in Pediatric Direct Primary Care. For Expanded Pediatric Care, please schedule a consultation; for PANS & PANDAS Care, please contact us to learn more.
