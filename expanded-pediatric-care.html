Expanded Pediatric Care

When Your Child Needs More.

Some health concerns don't fit neatly into a traditional pediatric visit.

Expanded Pediatric Care at Shipley Pediatrics provides families with ongoing, personalized support for children with complex, chronic, neurologic, developmental, or whole-child health concerns.

More time to listen. More time to investigate. More time to build a plan around your child.

Schedule a Consultation Who it's for

What is Expanded Pediatric Care?

Sometimes a child needs more than routine primary care.

Symptoms may overlap. Previous treatments may not have provided the answers you hoped for. Or you may simply feel there is more to understand about what's happening with your child.

Expanded Pediatric Care gives Jen the time and flexibility to take a deeper look at your child's health, history, symptoms, lifestyle, development, and overall well-being.

The goal isn't simply to address one symptom at a time. It's to better understand the whole picture and develop an individualized plan for your child.

It's about understanding your child—not simply treating a symptom.

Conditions & concerns

Who Is Expanded Pediatric Care For?

ADHD & ADD

Support for children with attention, focus, behavioral, executive-function, and related concerns.

Migraines & Headaches

Evaluation and ongoing management for children experiencing recurring headaches, migraines, or related symptoms.

POTS & Autonomic Concerns

Support for children and adolescents experiencing dizziness, fatigue, heart-rate changes, exercise intolerance, and other concerns related to autonomic function.

Neurologic Concerns

Jen's extensive pediatric neurology background provides an additional level of experience for families navigating neurologic symptoms and diagnoses.

Developmental & Behavioral Concerns

A broader look at development, behavior, learning, sleep, and other factors that may be affecting your child's day-to-day life.

Functional & Whole-Child Pediatric Care

An approach that considers how factors such as nutrition, sleep, gut health, lifestyle, environment, and overall wellness may contribute to your child's health.

Complex Pediatric Concerns

For children whose symptoms don't fit neatly into one category or families who feel they need more time and a broader perspective to understand what may be happening.

Looking at More Than a Diagnosis

At Shipley Pediatrics, we understand that children with complex concerns often don't fit into a single box.

A child's headaches may be connected to sleep. Fatigue may overlap with nutrition, hydration, stress, autonomic function, or another underlying concern. Attention challenges may exist alongside sleep, behavioral, developmental, or other health factors.

Expanded Pediatric Care allows Jen to take the time to look at these connections rather than focusing only on a diagnosis.

What does membership provide?

An ongoing care relationship, not a one-time consultation.

Depending on your child's individual needs, care may include:

  • Ongoing visits and follow-up
  • Extended time to discuss complex concerns
  • Review of medical history and previous care
  • Medication management when appropriate
  • Growth, development, nutrition, sleep, and lifestyle evaluation
  • Coordination with specialists and other members of your child's care team
  • Monitoring progress and adjusting the care plan over time

Your child's specific plan will depend on their individual health needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Expanded Pediatric Care Questions

Is Expanded Pediatric Care the same as Pediatric DPC?

No. Pediatric DPC is designed to provide your child's ongoing primary care. Expanded Pediatric Care is designed for children who need more specialized, in-depth, or ongoing support for complex health concerns.

Does Expanded Pediatric Care replace my child's pediatrician?

Not necessarily. Families may maintain their existing pediatrician or primary care provider while working with Jen for Expanded Pediatric Care.

Is this a one-time consultation?

Expanded Pediatric Care is structured as an ongoing monthly membership so Jen can follow your child's progress, make adjustments, and provide continuity over time.

Does membership include outside labs, imaging, medications, or specialists?

Services provided outside Shipley Pediatrics—including outside laboratory testing, imaging, medications, hospital services, and specialty care—are separate from the membership and may be billed by those providers or processed through your insurance.

Can Jen work with my child's other providers?

Care coordination can be an important part of managing complex pediatric concerns. When appropriate, Shipley Pediatrics can help coordinate with your child's primary care provider, specialists, therapists, and other members of their care team.

What if I'm not sure which Shipley service my child needs?

That's okay. Contact Shipley Pediatrics and tell us a little about what you're looking for. We'll help guide you toward the most appropriate next step.
