We're Here to Help

Whether you're looking for a new pediatric provider, seeking support for a more complex concern, or you're already part of the Shipley Pediatrics family, we'll help you find the right next step.

How Can We Help?

Pediatric DPC

I'm Interested in Pediatric Direct Primary Care

Looking for a pediatric provider who has more time to know your child and your family? Start with a complimentary Meet & Greet with Jennifer Shipley, CPNP.

This brief, non-clinical conversation gives you an opportunity to learn about Pediatric Direct Primary Care, ask questions, meet Jen, and decide whether Shipley Pediatrics feels like the right fit for your family.

Schedule a Meet & Greet

No commitment. No enrollment forms. Just a conversation.

Expanded Care

I'm Looking for Expanded Pediatric Care

If your child has complex, neurologic, developmental, behavioral, functional, or ongoing health concerns, Expanded Pediatric Care may be the appropriate place to start.

Contact our team to tell us a little about what you're looking for, and we'll help determine the appropriate next step.

Contact Us About Expanded Pediatric Care
PANS & PANDAS

I'm Looking for PANS & PANDAS Care

If your child has experienced sudden, significant changes and you're seeking evaluation or ongoing support for suspected or diagnosed PANS or PANDAS, we'd be happy to talk with you about Shipley's care options.

Contact Us About PANS & PANDAS Care
