A Different Approach to Pediatric Care

Every child is different. Their care should be, too.

Shipley Pediatrics combines comprehensive pediatric care with Jennifer Shipley’s extensive experience in pediatric neurology and complex pediatric conditions. From routine primary care to children who need a deeper level of evaluation and support, our goal is simple: thoughtful care, meaningful relationships, and a plan that makes sense for your child.

Three care pathways

Find the Right Care for Your Child

01 Primary care membership

Pediatric Direct Primary Care

A membership-based approach to primary care that gives families more time, better access, and an ongoing relationship with a provider who knows their child.

Routine care. Sick visits. Well-child care. Ongoing support. More time to ask questions and be heard.

Explore Pediatric DPC
02 Complex & whole-child care

Expanded Pediatric Care

When your child needs more than a traditional pediatric visit, we take the time to look deeper.

Expanded care is available for complex pediatric needs including functional and whole-child care, ADHD/ADD, migraines and headaches, POTS and autonomic concerns, neurologic conditions, developmental concerns, and more.

Explore Expanded Pediatric Care
03 Dedicated specialty pathway

PANS & PANDAS Care

When changes in your child feel sudden, significant, or difficult to explain, finding a provider who understands PANS and PANDAS can make a difference.

Shipley Pediatrics offers specialized evaluation and ongoing support for families navigating suspected or diagnosed PANS/PANDAS.

Learn About PANS & PANDAS Care
