Before you enroll

A note about insurance & DPC eligibility

Direct Primary Care is not health insurance, and we encourage families to maintain appropriate health insurance coverage for services outside of the DPC membership.

Patients covered by Blue Cross Blue Shield or Montana Medicaid are not currently eligible for Direct Primary Care membership while Shipley Pediatrics remains contracted with those plans.
